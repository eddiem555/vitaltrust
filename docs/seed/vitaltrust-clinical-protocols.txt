1. EMERGENCY ROOM (ER) CARDIAC ENCOUNTER PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Initial Triage & Vitals
- All presenting patients with acute chest pain, dyspnea, or syncope must receive a standard 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes of arrival.
- Baseline vitals collection mandate:
  * Blood Pressure (Systolic/Diastolic via automated cuff)
  * Continuous Pulse Oximetry (Target SpO2 > 94% on room air)
  * Core Body Temperature and Respiratory Rate
- High-risk biomarkers: Draw STAT Troponin I and CK-MB. Repeat troponin series at 3-hour and 6-hour intervals if initial markers remain non-diagnostic but clinical suspicion persists.

1.2 Pharmacological Interventions (Acute Coronary Syndrome)
- Administer Aspirin 324 mg chewable immediately unless an active anaphylactic allergy is documented in the EHR.
- Sublingual Nitroglycerin 0.4 mg every 5 minutes up to 3 doses, provided systolic BP > 100 mmHg and no right ventricular infarction is suspected.
- IV Heparin infusion per weight-based nomogram (80 units/kg bolus, followed by 18 units/kg/hr maintenance) titrated to therapeutic aPTT (60-80 seconds).


2. ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORD (EHR) ACCESS CONTROL & IDENTITY BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC)
- Attending Physicians & Residents: Full read/write access to clinical encounters, diagnostic imaging, pathology results, and active pharmacy orders. Step-up authentication (Cisco Duo Push) required when modifying narcotic regimens or signing discharge summaries.
- Nursing Staff: Read access to physician orders, allergies, and historical notes; write access to vitals observation logs, intake/output charts, and medication administration records (MAR).
- Administrative / Billing Staff: Restricted strictly to demographic and insurance billing records. All direct clinical charts, physician notes, and laboratory numerical outcomes are obfuscated.

2.2 ZTNA and Boundary Telemetry
- All backend application nodes (aiapp1 Frontend, aiapp2 MCP Host, aiapp3 PostgreSQL) require strict micro-segmentation.
- Direct database read/write queries are restricted strictly to authorized service identities via mutually authenticated channels.


3. SENSITIVE DATA GOVERNANCE & PHI BOUNDARIES (CISCO AI DEFENSE COMPLIANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Protected Health Information (PHI) Definition
Per HIPAA standards and VitalTrust Data Security Policy, the following identifiers must NEVER be ingested into un-sanctioned generative AI prompts or exported outside the AWS VPC boundary:
- Full Legal Name
- Social Security Numbers (SSN format: XXX-XX-XXXX)
- Medical Record Numbers (MRN format: MRN-XXXXXX)
- Date of Birth and residential address details
- Direct biometric telemetry or medical imaging DICOM headers

4. DATA RETENTION & SHADOW REPOSITORY POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Active clinical observations remain in high-availability PostgreSQL storage for 7 years post-discharge.
- Vector database embeddings generated for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) must reside exclusively inside private serverless OpenSearch collections.
- Temporary scratchpads, local LLM context buffers, and external cache layers must be flushed within 60 seconds of session termination.
